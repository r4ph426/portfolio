TeamBank · Stakeholder engagement microsites

Two microsites helped Tangity prepare for its first conversation with TeamBank after cold outreach and explore where the teams might work together.

The client-facing microsite gave the first meeting a structure. Seven questions helped guide the conversation and gauge where there might be an opportunity to collaborate. A separate internal microsite gave the Tangity team a place to prepare with relevant cases, team profiles and notes.

I designed both experiences around the same conversation. Each gave its audience the context it needed for a useful first exchange.

An introductory meeting benefits from a clear set of questions and a team that has prepared around them.
