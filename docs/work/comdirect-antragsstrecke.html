Digital banking · Application flow

comdirect · Application and onboarding

Explored how to simplify micro and standard onboarding in the comdirect app, including the transition from desktop to mobile.

I contributed to the redesign of the application flow across two onboarding paths. The work explored the journey at different levels, from where customers enter the flow to how the desktop experience hands off to the app. Figma documents a clickable prototype and explorations for home, quick access and the desktop handoff.

The entry point and the handoff are part of the application journey. Designing them together helps customers understand where to start and how to continue.
