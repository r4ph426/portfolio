B2B UX · Concept study

Deutsche Telekom · Cloud Marketplace concept study

Investigated where a B2B software marketplace could improve and built a prototype to make those opportunities tangible for discussion.

I mapped how business customers might buy software, provision it and manage licences. The investigation surfaced opportunities to clarify those journeys within the existing platform and design system constraints. I translated the opportunities into a concept prototype so stakeholders could compare approaches and discuss which improvements were worth pursuing.

A prototype gives stakeholders a concrete way to discuss improvement potential across a complex service.
