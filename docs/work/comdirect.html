UX/UI leadership · Digital banking

comdirect · Simplifying identity verification

A design sprint that turned a complex identity verification journey into a prototype stakeholders could review and validate.

Identity verification is a critical step in a banking application. The sprint focused on how the verification journey fit into the wider application and how to make that experience clearer.

As UX/UI Lead, I helped frame the problem, facilitate the sprint and translate the direction into a clickable prototype. The prototype gave stakeholders a concrete journey to walk through together and validate.

The sprint delivered a concept and prototype for stakeholder validation. It created a way to discuss the flow in context, including how applicants would enter and progress through verification.

A clickable journey makes it easier for stakeholders to discuss the sequence and handoffs they are asking customers to navigate.
